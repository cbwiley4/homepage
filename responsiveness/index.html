The Labrador Retriever, also known simply as the Labrador or Lab, is a British breed of retriever gun dog. It was developed in the United Kingdom from St John's water dogs imported from the colony of Newfoundland (now a province of Canada), and was named after the Labrador region in that colony. It is among the most commonly kept dogs in several countries, particularly in the Western world.
